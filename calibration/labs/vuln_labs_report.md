Counting and interpretation
- Do not score catalog rows as independent bugs: Juice Shop contains discovery/crypto/frontend tasks as well as vulnerabilities; DSVW source disclosure and traversal share a sink; several Juice Shop and bank exercises also share sites. Both row counts and unique supported locations are reported.
- A listed call on a vulnerable path is an upper bound on scanner visibility. XML/YAML failures inside fixed VM program strings are classified by their unsupported parser sinks, rather than by the incidental VM wrapper. JSON parsing before unsafe merging and constant commands used for log disclosure remain explicitly weak/adjacent cases.
- JSON parser entries are marked low signal in their notes. For a strict direct execution/path-injection benchmark, exclude weak wrapper/parser entries and the constant-command disclosure row rather than claiming detection recall from them.
- Source-located is not runtime-confirmed. reachable:yes means a visible static entry-to-sink path under the recorded preconditions; unknown is retained where the chain is not established. No exploit-success claim follows.
- fs.promises APIs are distinct from callback/sync fs APIs under the exact supplied list. If the builder later confirms a broader resolver contract, create a new manifest version; do not silently change this frozen key.
- Scope ratios are not exact fractions of all real vulnerabilities: catalog granularity is heterogeneous and unresolved rows can conceal alternative sink paths. Explicit discovery/usability tasks are retained with documented_vulnerability:false, and some remaining exercises still have ambiguous vulnerability granularity. No precision, recall, accuracy, or commercial readiness claim is made.
